🚀 An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing smartphone brand performance, revenue trends, pricing impact, and country-wise insights using Tableau.

---

## 🧠 Project Overview: 

This project provides a comprehensive analysis of smartphone sales data across multiple brands, countries, and payment modes.  
The goal is to identify revenue drivers, customer purchasing behavior, and strategic business insights through interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives:

✅ Analyze overall revenue & units sold  
✅ Compare brand-wise revenue contribution  
✅ Understand payment mode preferences  
✅ Evaluate pricing impact on sales volume  
✅ Track monthly revenue trends (2023–2025)  
✅ Identify top orders by revenue  
✅ Analyze country-wise brand performance  

---

## 📌 Key KPIs  

- 📈 Total Revenue: 3,268,607  
- 📦 Total Units Sold  
- 🌍 Countries Covered: India, USA, UK  
- 📱 Brands Analyzed: Apple, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Realme  
- 💳 Payment Modes: UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights:

### 🔹 Revenue & Sales Analysis  
- Credit Card & UPI contribute the highest revenue  
- Premium brands generate higher revenue with fewer units  

### 🔹 Brand-wise Revenue Contribution  
- 🥇 Apple & Samsung dominate total revenue  
- 📱 Oppo, Vivo, and Xiaomi contribute steady mid-range sales  

### 🔹 Impact of Pricing on Sales Volume  
- 📉 Higher prices lead to lower sales volume  
- 📊 Mid-range pricing shows maximum unit movement  

### 🔹 Monthly Revenue Trend (2023–2025)  
- Revenue shows seasonal spikes  
- Peak sales during festive & promotional periods  

### 🔹 Country-wise Performance  
- 🇮🇳 India shows high volume with competitive pricing  
- 🇺🇸 USA shows premium-driven revenue  
- 🇬🇧 UK shows stable brand distribution
